About

Dr. B. Damodaran, Ph.D.

Psychologist, Astrologer, Art of Living Faculty, educator and counsellor integrating traditional Jyotisha knowledge with contemporary psychological understanding.

At Sri Jyoti Astro Services, astrology is approached as a structured framework for reflection, timing and guidance—not merely as prediction. A consultation explores the birth chart, planetary periods and transits alongside your real-life context.

Dr. Damodaran’s background in psychology, psychometrics, counselling, trauma work, coaching and behavioural sciences adds a practical dimension to astrological interpretation, helping clients translate insight into thoughtful action.
